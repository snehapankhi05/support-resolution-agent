# Refund policy

The code in the eligibility service enforces these rules. This text is what a reviewer can cite.

## Delivered orders

A refund is possible only for a delivered order owned by the requesting customer.

## Return window

The customer must start the return within 30 days of delivery.

## Received and inspected

The return must be received by the warehouse and inspection must be passed.

## Refund amount

The refund amount is the paid amount of the returned items, and it cannot exceed the paid amount minus refunds already committed.

## Existing refund

An order that already has a reserved, processing, or succeeded refund for the same amount is not refunded again.

## Approval

Every refund requires approval by a reviewer. An approval expires after 24 hours.

## Failed inspection

If inspection failed, no refund is proposed and the case is escalated.

## Missing or conflicting policy

If no applicable policy can be found, or the policy evidence conflicts, the case is escalated.
